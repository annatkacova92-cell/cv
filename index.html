Side project

JobArea · AIRA

2023 – now · founder

AIRA (AI Resource Assistant) is a digital twin of a top headhunter. It matches people and companies the way a good recruiter does: from prescreening and intake conversations, not from keywords in a CV.

  • Runs candidate prescreens and company intakes, then matches on motivation, potential and team fit.
  • Remembers the full history with every candidate and company, so each search builds on the last one.
  • Gives both sides ongoing feedback, course recommendations and help with offer negotiation.
  • Free for job seekers; companies pay with packages or credits, well below agency fees.

Concept since 2023 and full product design in Figma. Next step: a working MVP and a pilot with real companies and candidates.
